Information Security Policy (Sample, v1.2)

1. Purpose. This policy sets out the company's approach to protecting information assets and supports our regulatory obligations.

2. Roles. The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is accountable for this policy. Department heads are responsible for compliance within their teams.

3. Access control. Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis and requires approval from the line manager. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access. User access rights are reviewed every six months.

4. Training. All employees must complete security awareness training when they join and once a year.

5. Data protection. Confidential data must be encrypted in transit. Removable media should be avoided where possible.

6. Incident reporting. Employees must report suspected security incidents to the IT service desk immediately.

7. Vendors. Third parties with access to company data must sign a confidentiality agreement.

8. Review. This policy is reviewed annually by the CISO.
