# NIST CSF 2.0 categories used by the app (22)

| ID | Function | Category | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| GV.OC | Govern | Organizational Context | Mission, stakeholder expectations, and legal/regulatory requirements that shape cybersecurity risk decisions are understood. |
| GV.RM | Govern | Risk Management Strategy | Risk appetite, tolerance, and the strategy for managing cybersecurity risk are established and communicated. |
| GV.RR | Govern | Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities | Cybersecurity roles, responsibilities, and authorities are established, communicated, and enforced. |
| GV.PO | Govern | Policy | Cybersecurity policy is established, communicated, enforced, and reviewed on a defined cycle. |
| GV.OV | Govern | Oversight | Results of cybersecurity risk management are reviewed to inform and adjust strategy. |
| GV.SC | Govern | Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management | Supply chain and third-party risks are identified, assessed, and managed. |
| ID.AM | Identify | Asset Management | Assets (hardware, software, data, services) are inventoried and managed by importance to objectives. |
| ID.RA | Identify | Risk Assessment | Cybersecurity risk to the organization, assets, and individuals is understood through threat, vulnerability, and impact analysis. |
| ID.IM | Identify | Improvement | Improvements to cybersecurity risk management are identified from evaluations, tests, and lessons learned. |
| PR.AA | Protect | Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control | Access to assets is limited to authorized users, services, and hardware and managed by risk. |
| PR.AT | Protect | Awareness and Training | Personnel are given cybersecurity awareness and role-based training. |
| PR.DS | Protect | Data Security | Data is managed to protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability (at rest, in transit, in use). |
| PR.PS | Protect | Platform Security | Hardware, software, and services are managed per risk (configuration, patching, logging, secure development). |
| PR.IR | Protect | Technology Infrastructure Resilience | Security architectures are managed to protect asset confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience. |
| DE.CM | Detect | Continuous Monitoring | Assets are monitored to find anomalies, indicators of compromise, and other adverse events. |
| DE.AE | Detect | Adverse Event Analysis | Anomalies and potential adverse events are analyzed to characterize them and detect incidents. |
| RS.MA | Respond | Incident Management | Responses to detected incidents are managed (triage, escalation, declaration). |
| RS.AN | Respond | Incident Analysis | Investigations support effective response and recovery, including forensics and root cause. |
| RS.CO | Respond | Incident Response Reporting and Communication | Response activities are coordinated with internal and external stakeholders. |
| RS.MI | Respond | Incident Mitigation | Activities are performed to prevent expansion of an event and mitigate its effects. |
| RC.RP | Recover | Incident Recovery Plan Execution | Restoration activities are performed to ensure operational availability of affected systems. |
| RC.CO | Recover | Incident Recovery Communication | Recovery activities and progress are communicated to stakeholders. |
